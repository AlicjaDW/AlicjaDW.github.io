Case study 01

Voice of the customer across a retail network

A customer feedback dashboard used by 50+ managers each month and adopted by teams across Europe.

Sector
Automotive and energy retail
Audience
Sales, service and delivery leaders
Tools
Python, JavaScript, HTML/CSS

Built with fictional sample data. The layout and logic match the working version.

Explore live dashboard

The result

2,500+ reviews across 100+ sites, used by 50+ managers and adopted by EU teams beyond the UK and Ireland.

The business question

Leaders across more than 100 showrooms, service centres and pop-ups received ratings and review text in a monthly spreadsheet. They couldn't see which sites were ahead, which had slipped, or what customers were actually saying.

What I built

  • One page that brings volume, rating and written feedback together, filtered by market, manager, location type and month.
  • An automatic plain-English summary of the month: who led, who slipped, and where to focus.
  • Praise grouped into themes such as helpful staff, test drive experience and good communication, each with a representative quote.
  • Every 1 to 3 star comment listed for follow-up, plus a site-level call to action when a single location is selected.
  • A ranking of the colleagues customers name most often in 4 and 5 star reviews.

What changed

Managers coach on the themes customers write about, not just the score. Low-rated reviews are now followed up while they're still recoverable.

Why it matters in luxury retail

Clienteling depends on knowing what each client felt in store. This is the same work: turning feedback into site-level actions and recognising the advisors clients praise by name.
